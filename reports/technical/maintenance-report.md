# Maintenance report

Use to record a maintenance intervention and the evidence needed for a return-to-service decision. Copy the [technical template](../templates/technical-report-template.md).

Record equipment ID, initial symptom, work scope/authorization, isolation procedure reference where applicable, tasks performed, parts/configuration changes, personnel/date, observations, tests and residual issues.
Keep suspected cause separate from a confirmed failure mechanism. Reference a [test report](test-report.md) for post-maintenance checks with acceptance criteria.

Link the [inventory record](../../inventory/README.md), [action](../../process-management/5w2h/README.md) and [document revision](../../document-control/revision-control.md).
Do not advance inspection dates or mark equipment available without the appropriate evidence and decision. Planning future work is not a maintenance result.
