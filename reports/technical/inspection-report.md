# Inspection report

Use to document an observed condition against identified criteria. Copy the [technical template](../templates/technical-report-template.md).

Record inspection ID/date, scope, inspector/reviewer, inventory equipment ID and revision/configuration, method, criterion source, observations and evidence references. State which areas were inaccessible or not inspected.
Separate observations from suspected causes and recommendations. Include photos or measurements when they substantiate the finding.

Link [Inventory](../../inventory/README.md) for identity and dates; create a [5W2H action](../../process-management/5w2h/README.md) for a reviewed finding and consider [FMEA](../../quality/fmea/README.md) when a structured risk review is useful.
The inventory validator only flags record conditions; it cannot perform an inspection or authorize equipment release.
