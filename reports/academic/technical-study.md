# Academic technical study

Use for research, analytical comparisons, simulation studies or exploratory engineering investigations.
Copy the [academic template](../templates/academic-report-template.md) and use the [analysis template](../templates/engineering-analysis-template.md) for model details.

Define the research question, literature selection, input-data provenance, assumptions, model, software/version, parameters and comparison method.
For simulations, document boundary/initial conditions, mesh/time step where applicable, convergence/sensitivity checks and relevant experimental/reference validation. Reproducing a solver output does not establish physical validity.
For experiments, record instrumentation, uncertainty, repeats and deviations. For a literature-only study, clearly mark that no original measurements were collected.

Cite only sources actually used; distinguish verified findings from proposals and unanswered questions.
