# Standards and compliance references

These CSV files are **reference indexes**, not compliance checklists. No standards text or PDFs are included. Confirm current edition, national adoption, scope, and applicability directly with the official publisher or regulator before use. A listing does not imply certification, compliance, or legal advice.

Official starting points:

- ABNT catalog: https://www.abntcatalogo.com.br/
- ISO standards catalogue: https://www.iso.org/standards.html
- Brazilian Ministry of Labor and Employment, Regulatory Standards: https://www.gov.br/trabalho-e-emprego/pt-br/assuntos/inspecao-do-trabalho/seguranca-e-saude-no-trabalho/ctpp-nrs/normas-regulamentadoras-nrs

Indexes record source links and explicitly mark edition/status as requiring confirmation. Do not distribute copyrighted standards documents through this project.
