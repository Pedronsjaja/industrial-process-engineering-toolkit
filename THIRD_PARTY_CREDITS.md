# Third-party credits and references

No third-party code, templates, spreadsheets, documentation, or datasets are bundled in this initial implementation. The project structure and example data are original and fictional.

External standards and methodological references are pointers only; no protected standards text is reproduced. See the official-source URLs in `standards/README.md` and the standards indexes.

Before incorporating third-party material, contributors must verify its license, comply with attribution/share-alike obligations, and record the source and reused material here.
