# Third-party credits and references

No third-party code, templates, spreadsheets, documentation, or operational datasets are currently bundled in this implementation. The standards indexes contain brief reference metadata and official source links, not copied standards text. The included project structure and example data are presented as original project work; all example operational records are fictional.

External methodological and standards references are pointers only; a reference does not mean that source content has been reproduced. No protected standards text is included. Official-source links are listed in `standards/README.md` and the standards indexes.

Before incorporating third-party material, contributors must verify its license, comply with attribution and share-alike obligations, and add an entry here containing:

- Project or source name and URL.
- License and the date/version checked, when known.
- Component or material reused.
- Changes made to the reused material.
- Required attribution and where it is presented.

No legal advice is provided; contributors are responsible for checking the applicable license terms.
